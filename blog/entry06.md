### Takeaways from In-class Presentation and Expo Elevator Pitch

One of the biggest takeaways I got from my presentation was **Adapting to the Room**. While I was up presentinig I could see that some of my classmates getting confused when I was explaining the solution to my challenge for my freedom project, so I immediately started explaining what render was and what it did to help run and make my game work on github pages. After explaining what it did, I could see people start to understand the solution to my challenge. Another big takeaway I got from my presentation was the **Importance of Explaining**. I can tell that only after explaining my project and code more throughly that people started to understand and get what I was trying to explain to them. This allowed for my audience to have a simplier time understanding and learning all about the tools I used and problems I came by with them.

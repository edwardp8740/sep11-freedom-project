### Takeaways from In-class Presentation and Expo Elevator Pitch

One of the biggest takeaways I got from my presentation was **Adapting to the Room**. While I was up presentinig I could see that some of my classmates getting confused when I was explaining the solution to my challenge for my freedom project, so I immediately started explaining what render was and what it did to help run and make my game work on github pages. After explaining what it did, I could see people start to understand the solution to my challenge. Another big takeaway I got from my presentation was the **Importance of Explaining**. I can tell that only after explaining my project and code more throughly that people started to understand and get what I was trying to explain to them. This allowed for my audience to have a simplier time understanding and learning all about the tools I used and problems I came by with them.

One of my biggest takeaways from my expo elevator pitch is **structure** and **preparation**. I think my pitch lacked a bit of structuce and preparation, I had a scripted but got nervous to say the script how I planned. This led to mistakes and a few repeats of what I had to say. A more structuced and prepared expo pitch would've been better. I would've been able to talk more about my product and said everything in less and simplier words. I think I should've sticked to my script just a bit more than only trying to summarize it, which is what led to these mistakes.


### Skills

Two skills I’ve improved on are **Collaboration** and **Communication**. One of the biggest things about making a game meant to help with study and keep it fun, is to consider what a student needs to learn or help understand better. This whole project is about consideration for others, how it could be fun for students, how they can learn, etc. Working with a partner is difficult and people need to communicate ahead of time. We got some help from our peers, but even talking with other people outside of SEP, such as our AP Chem teacher for help to get questions and other stuff that could help with studying about AP Chem.
